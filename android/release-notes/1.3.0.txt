What's new in 1.3.0:

• Cyberpunk theme — A retro-futuristic "ROAM-OS handheld" look with CRT scanlines, film grain, and a phosphor-teal and gold palette.
• Theme switcher — Pick between Roam Classic (the original look) and the new Cyberpunk theme in Settings → Appearance.
• Device frame — Frame the screen in a handheld chassis, or go frameless for a minimal look.
• CRT effects toggle — Turn scanlines and grain on or off.
• New boot/scan loading screen — A retro scan sequence with themed messages that completes before each page loads.
• OS status bar — See the current category, subcategory, and domain of each page at a glance.
