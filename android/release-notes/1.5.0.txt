What's new in 1.5.0:

• In-device programs — A "cartridges" shelf you can open from the config sheet. The five arcade programs run inside the handheld: RELAY, BOTFIELD, COMPILER, DEBRIS and FIREWALK.
• RELAY, BOTFIELD, COMPILER, DEBRIS and FIREWALK — Arcade cartridges with a boot-time controls guide, swipe or on-screen pad, a level loop, and an XP award on every clear.
• Background return fix — Coming back from the lock screen or another app no longer leaves Roam stuck on the boot screen. The page reappears straight away, and if Android killed the page while you were away Roam rebuilds it and puts you back where you were.
