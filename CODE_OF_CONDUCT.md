# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Roam to be a welcoming project for everyone. We pledge to make
participation harassment-free regardless of background, identity, or experience
level.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, PRs, and discussions.
- Assume good faith and disagree with ideas, not people.
- Give and receive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, hate speech, or discrimination of any kind.
- Personal attacks, insults, or trolling.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Spam or sustained off-topic posting.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it by email to
`developer@roamtheweb.app`. Reports are reviewed promptly and handled
confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, issues, or contributions that violate this
code, and may temporarily or permanently ban repeat offenders.
