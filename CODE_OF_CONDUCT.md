# Code of Conduct

Roam is an open project and contributors are expected to behave with basic respect and restraint.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and professional.
- Critique ideas, not people.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.
- Avoid harassment, intimidation, or exclusionary language.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Hate speech or discriminatory comments.
- Deliberate disruption of collaboration.
- Sharing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may delete harmful content, close discussions, or reject contributions that violate this code of conduct.
