# Subject: We fixed some stuff. Try not to make a big deal about it.

Hey there,

Another batch of changes shipped to the Android app and browser extension. Nothing life-changing, but your phone and browser are now slightly less annoying to interact with. Congratulations.

What's different, in descending order of how much you'll actually notice:

**The algorithm no longer has a favorite blog**
Discovery now rotates through more subcategories instead of settling into a rut. It also takes a 24-hour breather on domains you just visited, so you won't get served three different articles from the same food blog in a single session. Sometimes it throws in a complete random curveball just to see what you do. We call it "serendipity mode." Marketing made us say that.

**Blank screens are officially canceled**
If Android murders the app in the background to free up RAM for some garbage mobile game you installed six months ago, Roam now remembers exactly where you were and restores it. Previous behavior: blank white screen. New behavior: the page you were actually reading. Bold strategy, we know.

**You can now copy a URL without performing a ritual**
Swipe up the config sheet. There it is. Tap to copy. No more share menu spelunking. You're welcome.

**Collections now load when you open the sheet**
Groundbreaking innovation: we fetch your collections when you open the config sheet, instead of hoping a stale cache from three sessions ago is still accurate. The "Add to collection" picker now shows what you actually have. Incredible stuff.

**The browser extension works on Firefox again**
Firefox users couldn't sign in. This was not a strategic decision. It was a bug. Specifically, Firefox handles `postMessage` differently than Chrome, and we handle that differently now. Chrome got a backup sign-in path too, because paranoia is a virtue. Also, pages load faster because we stopped doing a bunch of unnecessary work in the background. Everybody wins.

**Everything is slightly faster**
Batch URL fetching, smarter caching, unified request plumbing across all platforms. You won't notice this paragraph but your cellular data plan will appreciate it.
