# Roam Update — v1.0.12 Is Here! 🚀

Hi Roamers,

We just shipped Roam v1.0.12 with some quality-of-life improvements to make your browsing smoother and faster. Here's what's new:

---

## What's New

- **Translate button** — Tapping the new **"Translate this page"** button translates the page on the spot. We've replaced the old auto-translate toggle with this more reliable, one-tap approach.

- **Faster page loading** — We now proactively warm up the next page in the background while you're reading, and the loading spinner dismisses earlier so you can start reading sooner.

- **Your scroll position is safe** — Switching apps or locking your screen no longer loses your place on the page. Scroll position is now preserved across app backgrounding and resuming.

- **Smoother navigation** — Fixed a bug where the back and forward buttons would get stuck on the same page. Browsing history now works naturally, just like it should.

- **Quicker queue refill** — You'll spend less time waiting between pages, and more time discovering.

---

## Already on 1.0.12?

Your app should have updated automatically. If not, you can grab the latest version from the [Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.roam.android).

Thanks for being part of Roam — we're building something special, and your feedback helps us make it better with every release.
