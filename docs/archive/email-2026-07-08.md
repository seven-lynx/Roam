## 🛡️ XP System: We Fixed the Economy

Remember how some of you were gaming the XP system? Yeah, we noticed. We're not mad — we're just *disappointed*. And we fixed it.

- **Anti-farming protections** — Cooldowns now prevent rapid-fire XP grinding. You'll have to earn it the old-fashioned way: by actually using the app.
- **Level sync trigger** — Your level now stays in sync with your XP automatically. No more "I swear I should be level 12" moments.
- **Level-up notifications restored** — They were broken. They work now. You'll know when you level up.

## ⚡ Performance: Faster Everything

We did some backend spring cleaning:

- **Polling → Realtime** — Things update when they actually change, not when we guess they might have changed. Your battery thanks us.
- **Badge evaluation decoupled** — Badges now evaluate independently instead of blocking everything else. Opening the app no longer feels like launching a space shuttle.
- **Database indexes** — We added indexes to columns we query a lot. If that sentence means nothing to you, just know: it's faster now.
