## 🔥 The Big One: Social Features (v1.1.0)

Roam now supports the full range of human emotions: admiration (follow), indifference (unfollow), curiosity (view profile), and the deep-seated need to say "look at this weird thing I found" (share URL with a friend).

**Specifically:**
- **Follow/unfollow users** — Find someone whose taste you respect? Follow them. They post something terrible? Unfollow them. No hard feelings.
- **Public profiles** — Tap any username to see their bio, badges, collections, and follower counts. Judge them accordingly.
- **Activity feed** — See what the people you follow are discovering, rating, and collecting. It's like a news feed, but for web pages instead of your uncle's political opinions.
- **Share with a friend** — Found something weird? Send it directly to another Roam user. They'll get a push notification. You'll get the satisfaction of ruining their productivity.
- **User search** — Type a username and find people. We don't have an algorithm for this. You just... type.
- **Follower/following lists** — Tap the counts on your profile to see who's following you (hi mom) and who you're following.

---

## 👤 A New "You" Tab

The bottom bar used to say "Settings." It now says "You." Because really, isn't that what you came here for?

The You tab shows your **level, XP, streak, follower/following counts, and badges** at a glance. Everything else — profile editing, saved URLs, history, notifications, collections — is one tap away. Settings still exists, but it's been gracefully demoted to a link inside the You tab, where it belongs.

---

## 🏆 Leaderboard Improvements

- **Tap any row → view their profile.** The leaderboard is no longer a wall of unclickable names taunting you with their superior XP. You can now stalk top explorers properly.
- **Top 3 styling works in dark mode.** Previously, the gold/silver/bronze highlights used a color that looked great in light mode and completely illegible in dark mode. We fixed it. Your retinas are safe.
- **Private profiles no longer appear in rankings.** If someone wants to lurk invisibly, we respect that.

---

## 🐛 Bugs We Murdered

- **Leaderboard crash** — Tapping the leaderboard tab previously threw a cryptic JSON error. We were trying to parse a whole object as an array. We are not proud of this.
- **Badges showing 0** — The app was looking in the wrong table. Badges now correctly show your actual progress via the proper database function. You probably have more badges than you thought. Surprise!
- **Activity feed button did nothing** — The button was there, just... existentially non-functional. It now takes you to an actual activity feed. Revolutionary.
- **Duplicate URLs in discovery** — The discovery algorithm had been replaced with a stub that didn't track what you'd already seen. You were getting the same URLs on repeat, Groundhog Day style. Fixed.
- **User search now respects privacy** — Only public profiles show up in search results. Private profiles stay private. As the name implies.
- **Config bottom sheet got streamlined** — "Category preferences" now opens natively in the app instead of launching the web version. "Share with a friend" is a new button. We also removed a couple of redundant links. Marie Kondo would be proud.

## 🔧 Other Stuff Worth Mentioning

- **WebView now handles cleartext HTTP** — Some websites still live in 2005. We now accommodate them instead of showing a blank screen and pretending it's their fault.
- **Scroll position preserved on background** — Switch apps, lock your phone, take a call — when you come back, you'll be exactly where you left off. No more losing your place in a 5,000-word article.
- **"Open in Roam Web"** — Opens roamtheweb.app inside the app with automatic sign-in. Your session cookie is injected so you don't have to log in again. Magic? No. Cookies? Yes.
- **Backend activity tracking** — We shipped a new `user_activity` table with automatic triggers on ratings, submissions, and collection creation. This powers the activity feed across all platforms. It's the kind of infrastructure work nobody gets excited about but everyone benefits from.
